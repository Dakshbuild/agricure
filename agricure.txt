Step 1: Create the GitHub Repository
1. Go to https://github.com and log in (or create a free account).
2. Click the "+" icon in the top right corner and select "New repository".
3. Name your repository "agricure".
4. Select "Private" (so no one else can see your code).
5. Click the green "Create repository" button.

Step 2: Upload Your Code
1. On the next screen, look for the small link that says "uploading an existing file" and click it.
2. Open your computer's File Explorer and go to this folder: 
   C:\Users\Hp\OneDrive\Desktop\test\agricure
3. Select the files and folders inside and drag them into the GitHub page.
   CRITICAL WARNING: Do NOT upload the "node_modules" or ".next" folders! They are massive and will crash your upload. Vercel will generate these automatically later.
4. Once the files finish uploading, scroll down and click the green "Commit changes" button.

Step 3: Deploy to Vercel
1. Go to https://vercel.com and sign up/log in using the "Continue with GitHub" option.
2. Click the black "Add New..." button and select "Project".
3. You will see your new "agricure" repository listed there. Click the "Import" button next to it.
4. Leave all settings exactly as they are and click the big "Deploy" button.

Vercel will now build your site. In about 2 minutes, you will be given a permanent, live URL that you can share with your sir. It will stay online for as long as you need!
